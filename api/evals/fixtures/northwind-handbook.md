# Northwind Labs Employee Handbook

Northwind Labs is a fictional software company. This handbook is a made-up document used to evaluate Chatwave's search and answers. None of it describes a real employer.

## Working hours and remote work

Our core hours are 10:00 to 16:00, Paris time. Outside core hours, you organise your day as you like, as long as your team knows when to reach you. Meetings are never scheduled before 9:30 or after 17:30.

Everyone may work remotely up to three days a week. Teams agree on one shared office day so people actually meet. Working from another country is allowed for up to 20 working days per calendar year, after telling your manager and the People team at least two weeks before leaving.

If you need to be offline during core hours (a medical appointment, a school run), set your status in the team chat. There is no need to justify it.

## Paid leave

Full-time employees get 25 days of paid vacation per year, plus public holidays. Part-time employees get the same number of weeks, prorated in days.

Up to 5 unused vacation days can be carried over to the next year. Carried-over days must be taken before March 31; after that date they are lost. Ask your manager for leave at least two weeks ahead for anything longer than three days.

Sick days do not count against vacation. From the third consecutive sick day, send a medical certificate to the People team.

## Parental leave

Every new parent, whatever their gender and however the child joins the family (birth, adoption or surrogacy), gets 16 weeks of fully paid parental leave. It can be taken in up to three blocks during the child's first year. During the first month back, parents may work 80% of their usual hours at full pay.

## Expenses

Business expenses are reimbursed through Ledgerly, our expense tool. Submit each expense with a photo of the receipt within 30 days; later claims need your manager's written approval.

Meals during business trips are covered up to 45 euros per day. Alcohol is never reimbursed. Taxis are fine after 21:00 or when public transport would take more than twice as long. For travel rules, see the separate travel policy.

## Equipment

Every employee chooses a laptop on their first day, and laptops are refreshed every three years. New employees also receive a one-time home-office stipend of 400 euros, for a chair, a desk or a screen. Broken equipment is repaired or replaced at no cost; report it to the IT desk.

## Security

Use Vaultbox, the company password manager, for every work password. Multi-factor authentication is mandatory on every work account, without exception.

If you lose a device or suspect a security incident, report it to the security team within one hour, by writing to the #security-alerts channel or calling the on-call number on the intranet. Reporting quickly matters more than being sure: false alarms are welcome.

## Health insurance

Northwind Labs pays 100% of the health insurance for employees and 50% for their family members, with Helvia as the insurer. Dental and optical care are included in the standard plan. Join the plan during your first month; afterwards, changes are only possible each January or after a life event such as a birth or a marriage.

## Office and facilities

The Paris office is on the fourth floor of 12 rue des Lilas. Badges are handed out on your first day and must be worn inside the building. The office is open from 7:30 to 21:00 on working days; weekend access needs a request to the facilities team.

There is secure bike parking in the courtyard and a shower on each floor. Meeting rooms are booked through the intranet; a room booked but unused for 15 minutes is released automatically.

The office closes between Christmas and New Year. Those days are given on top of your 25 vacation days, not taken from them.

## Learning and growth

Each employee has a learning budget of 1,200 euros per year for books, courses and conferences, plus two learning days a year to spend on them. The budget does not roll over.

Performance reviews happen twice a year, in May and in November. Each review starts with a self-assessment, followed by a conversation with your manager; peers can be invited to give feedback.

## Referrals

If someone you recommended is hired, you receive a referral bonus of 2,000 euros, paid once the new colleague has completed six months. Referrals for internships are thanked with a dinner for two instead.

## Code of conduct

We expect kindness, honesty and respect, in the office, online and at company events. Harassment and discrimination are never tolerated. Concerns can be raised with your manager, the People team, or anonymously through the Speakup form; every report is answered within five working days.

## Onboarding

Every new hire is paired with an onboarding buddy from another team for their first six weeks. The buddy is the person to ask the questions you think are too small to ask anyone else. Your manager sends a 30-60-90 day plan before your first day.
